Model assumptions & calibration

Each long finger moves around two joint axes derived from the STEP geometry. The bending distribution is uncalibrated. The manual defines overall angle α limits of 20°–176°, but no individual joint curve. Intermediate values are assumed linear. Thumb axis candidates come from CAD; limits of −13°–70° (bending) and 90°–165° (rotation) come from the manual. Motion directions and motor-value mappings are assumptions.

CAD surfaces at part boundaries are assigned by spatial segmentation. Small gaps or overlaps may be visible. This viewer is not a validated mechanical simulator.

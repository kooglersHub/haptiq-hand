Nearby contacts and hand limits share one representative within this spacing; 0 keeps all points. Opposing hand surfaces remain separate. A thumb at least 85% closed with measured thumb contact gives a provisional smaller-sensor first guess. Known sensors are fitted near their usual in-hand placement, with limited position and angle adjustments. Quick uses a short local search; Detailed tries additional local starts. A provisional placement does not confirm sensor identity. Known-sensor fits check exact contact rays and sampled penetration into closed hand CAD parts at the frozen pose and selected recorded finger/thumb poses. Similar fits require an explicit model choice for comparison. Open hand parts and continuous collision clearance remain unverified. The hand allowance defaults to 3 mm. Detailed fitting tries several palm-aligned orientations, then refines rotation and translation. Hand limits guide the search and use conservative local surface patches requiring calibrated geometry.
